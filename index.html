What changed

The podcast was the beginning. The road is the show.

Instead of asking "who can we interview this week," we ask: whose story should we visit, experience, and show next?

Interviews remain — they're the Industry Briefing now. The main attraction is original field production: facilities, fleets, departures, destinations, and the people who make a trip work. Every feature becomes durable marketing for the operator and a permanent entry in a growing visual record of the industry.

What a shoot day looks like

One day on site. Minimal disruption.

We arrive the evening before for a walkthrough and paperwork, then shoot a single day built around your real schedule — not a staged one.

  • Golden hour twice: the morning pull-out and the evening return.
  • Three or four short conversations with people you choose.
  • PPE, insurance certificate, and signed releases handled before we film.
  • You receive a recruiting cut, a sales cut, vertical clips, and a stills set.

Fleet & Innovation

New deliveries, new technology, and the long view.

Coach reveals and interior walk-throughs, accessibility and lift systems, driver-assistance and safety technology, electrification pilots, telematics, and the maintenance practices that keep a million-dollar asset earning. Fleet investment is moving again — new and pre-owned sales rose 7.9% year over year in the first quarter of 2026 (American Bus Association).

  • Delivery-day films for operators taking new equipment.
  • Manufacturer and supplier segments, clearly labeled as sponsored.
  • Accessibility coverage that treats it as engineering, not a checkbox.

About

We travel to tell the stories the industry deserves to have told.

The Motorcoach Channel is produced and hosted by Ryan Kelly of Terrapin Blue. It began as a podcast built on conversations with operators, association leaders, manufacturers, and destinations. It continues as a field production company with a camera in the garage, on the coach, and at the destination.

The goal is a permanent, public visual archive of how America moves by motorcoach — one that helps operators recruit and market, gives the industry a face outside its own trade press, and shows travelers what a professional operation actually looks like.
